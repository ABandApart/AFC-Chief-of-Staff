# Template 10: The Hiring Trigger

**Section:** Series A

**When to use it:** The strongest Series A angle you have. Use once the req has been open thirty days or more.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Saw you are hiring a [Role Title]

```
Hi [First Name],

Noticed [Company Name] is hiring a [Role Title]. Posted about [X] weeks ago, if I am reading the date right.

Searches at that level run ninety to a hundred and twenty days, then ramp. That is two quarters where [function] is effectively unled, during the two quarters you raised the money to accelerate.

I run [function] fractionally and most of my engagements start exactly here. Hold the function, build what the permanent hire inherits, and help you scope and interview so the search lands better. Clean exit the day they start.

Worth fifteen minutes before you get to a shortlist?

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

Referencing the posting date is what makes this land. It proves you looked, and it puts the timeline problem in their head without you having to argue for it.

## Failure Mode

Positioning yourself as an alternative to the hire. The offer is until the hire and helping make the hire better. Get this wrong and you have created an enemy in the recruiter and a defensive founder in the same email.
