# Touch 3: Touch 3: The Proof

**Section:** Follow Up Sequence (Touches 2-5)

**When to use it:** Days 14 to 30. New subject line, breaking the thread on purpose.

## Template Text

**Subject:** How [Similar Company] handled [function]

```
Hi [First Name],

Changing the subject line because the last one has probably sunk.

[Similar Company] was roughly where [Company Name] is about [timeframe] ago. Nobody owning [function], team growing underneath it, everything technically fine until it was not.

I came in fractionally and [specific outcome with a number] within [timeframe].

I wrote up how it actually worked. Want it?

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

Breaking the thread here is deliberate. Three messages deep on one subject line is a thread they have already decided to ignore. A new subject gets a fresh open decision.

## Failure Mode

Attaching the write-up instead of offering it. The yes is the engagement. An unrequested attachment is a brochure.
