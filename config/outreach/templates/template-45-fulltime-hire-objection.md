# Template 45: The Full Time Hire Objection

**Section:** Handling the Reply

**When to use it:** "We are hiring for this permanently." The most common objection you will face.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Re: [Original Subject]

```
Hi [First Name],

Good, and you should. A permanent [Role Title] is the right end state and I am not trying to talk you out of it.

The question is what happens to [function] between now and that person's first ninety days, which is realistically [X] months out. I hold it until then, build what they inherit, and help you scope the role so the search lands better. I exit the day they start.

If it is useful either way, I can tell you what I would look for in that hire.

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

Agreeing with the objection immediately is what makes this work. The founder is braced for an argument about why they do not need the permanent hire, and not receiving one changes the conversation.

## Failure Mode

Arguing that fractional is better than full time. You will lose, and you will deserve to.
