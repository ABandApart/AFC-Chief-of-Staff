# Template 44: Answering and Advancing

**Section:** Handling the Reply

**When to use it:** A reply containing a substantive question.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Re: [Original Subject]

```
Hi [First Name],

[Direct and complete answer to their question. Two to four sentences. Do not hold anything back for the call.]

There is more underneath that, particularly around [specific topic] and [specific topic], but that is the short version.

Would fifteen minutes this week be useful?

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

Answer it fully. A vendor withholds detail to force a demo because the product is the value. For a fractional the judgment is the value, so demonstrating it in the reply is the sale. Students who withhold here are copying a playbook written for a different business.

## Failure Mode

Answering a question you are not sure about. At this level a confident wrong answer ends the process, and I do not know but here is how I would find out is a genuinely strong reply.
