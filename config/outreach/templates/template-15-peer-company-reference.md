# Template 15: The Peer Company Reference

**Section:** Series A

**When to use it:** Anytime you have a genuine same-stage, same-sector engagement to point at.

## Template Text

**Subject:** What [Peer Company] did differently after their A

```
Hi [First Name],

[Peer Company] raised their A around the same time last year. One of the first moves they made was bringing in a fractional [function] instead of running a six month search for the permanent one.

[Specific outcome: they went from X to Y in Z months, and hired the full time [role] in month eight with a far clearer brief than they would have written in month one.]

I am not saying [Company Name] should do the same thing. Your situation is not theirs.

But the parallels are close enough to be worth twenty minutes.

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

The disclaimer is not softening, it is credibility. Founders are highly allergic to being told what another company did as though it settles anything.

## Failure Mode

Naming a peer company you did not actually work with. At Series A the sector is small enough that this gets checked in one message.
