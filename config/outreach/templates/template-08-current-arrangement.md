# Template 08: The Current Arrangement

**Section:** Seed Stage

**When to use it:** Replaces the competitor displacement angle. A fractional does not displace a tool, they displace whatever is holding the function together right now.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Who owns [function] at [Company Name]?

```
Hi [First Name],

Best guess from the outside: [function] at [Company Name] is currently you, an agency, or someone junior doing their best with it.

All three work for a while. They usually stop working around [trigger: the first board meeting where someone asks for the number / the second deal that stalls with nobody senior to own it].

I run [function] fractionally. Senior judgment on a defined term, without a full time hire you are not ready to make.

Worth fifteen minutes?

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

Guessing out loud is the mechanic. If you are right, you have demonstrated you understand their business. If you are wrong, they will correct you, and a correction is a reply.

## Failure Mode

Naming the agency or the junior person specifically. Keep it general. The moment it gets personal you have made an enemy inside the company.
