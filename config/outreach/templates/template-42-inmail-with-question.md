# Template 42: The InMail With a Question

**Section:** LinkedIn

**When to use it:** Alternative InMail when you have no named proof in their sector yet.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Quick question about [Company Name]

```
Hi [First Name],

Curious who owns [function] at [Company Name] since the round. At your stage the answer is usually you, or nobody yet.

I run [function] fractionally and I have a view on how to sequence it before the headcount lands.

Worth ten minutes?

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

This is the best template in the pack for a student without a case study library, because it leads with a question and a point of view rather than proof.

## Failure Mode

Asking a question you could have answered from their team page. If the answer is public, you look like you did not look.
