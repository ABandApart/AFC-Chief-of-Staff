# Touch 4: Touch 4: The Bridge

**Section:** Follow Up Sequence (Touches 2-5)

**When to use it:** Days 30 to 45. The only touch in the sequence that asks directly.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Re: [Original Subject]

```
Hi [First Name],

Last substantive one from me for a while.

The arithmetic I keep coming back to: [Role Title] searches run ninety to a hundred and twenty days, then ramp. If the req went up in [month], [function] is effectively unled through [quarter]. That is the quarter the raise was meant to accelerate.

What I do is hold the function on a defined term, build what the permanent hire inherits, help you scope and interview for the role, and exit the day they start.

Fifteen minutes?

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

Doing the arithmetic out loud is more persuasive than asserting urgency, because the founder finishes the calculation themselves and reaches your conclusion without being pushed to it.

## Failure Mode

Manufacturing scarcity. You have no deadline and they know it. The timeline pressure is theirs, not yours, and pretending otherwise costs you the peer positioning you spent three touches building.
