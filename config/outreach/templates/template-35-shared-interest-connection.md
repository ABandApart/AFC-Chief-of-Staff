# Template 35: The Shared Interest Connection

**Section:** LinkedIn

**When to use it:** When they publish regularly and you have genuinely read it.

## Template Text

**Subject:** LinkedIn message, no subject line

```
Hi [First Name], we are both in the [industry] world. Your post on [topic] was the first thing I have read on it that matched what I actually saw running [function] at [Company]. Would like to connect.
```

## Pro Tip

The second clause is the whole request. Agreement is cheap and everybody sends it. Agreement backed by having done the job is not, and it establishes what you are before they open your profile.

## Failure Mode

Referencing a post you skimmed. If they reply asking what you meant, and they sometimes do, you need an answer.
