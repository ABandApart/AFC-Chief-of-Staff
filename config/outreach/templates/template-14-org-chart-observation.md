# Template 14: The Org Chart Observation

**Section:** Series A

**When to use it:** Replaces the tech stack observation. Use when the team page and open roles reveal the shape of the leadership layer.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Your [function] structure

```
Hi [First Name],

Reading your team page and open roles, it looks like [Company Name] has [observation: three account executives and a sales ops hire with the VP seat still open / a controller but nobody above them on finance].

That is a normal Series A shape and it works until [trigger: forecasting becomes a board conversation / the first enterprise deal needs someone senior in the room].

I run [function] fractionally at exactly that shape. I sit above [the existing layer], own the number, and develop [the existing person] toward the seat if that is where it should go.

Want me to show you how that has worked elsewhere?

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

The development line is the most important sentence in the entire Series A pack. It converts the person already in the function from your biggest blocker into your advocate.

## Failure Mode

Reading the org chart out loud in a way that sounds like an audit. One observation, then move to what you would do about it.
