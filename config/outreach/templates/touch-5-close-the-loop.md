# Touch 5: Touch 5: Close the Loop

**Section:** Follow Up Sequence (Touches 2-5)

**When to use it:** Days 60 to 90. Breakup and re-engagement in a single message.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Closing the loop, [First Name]

```
Hi [First Name],

I have reached out a few times and I do not want to keep landing in your inbox, so I am closing this out on my end.

One thing before I do. You are roughly two months past the raise now, which usually means the first real numbers exist and [the search / the gap] looks different than it did in [month]. If either of those is true, this is a different conversation and I am easy to find.

Wishing [Company Name] well either way.

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

This is the highest converting message in the sequence and the one students skip because the first four produced silence. It works because it does two things at once: the window is visibly closing, and the evidence they did not have in month one now exists.

## Failure Mode

Any trace of resentment. Read it back and if there is a hint of you have not replied to me in it, rewrite. Aggrieved is the one tone that guarantees no reply.
