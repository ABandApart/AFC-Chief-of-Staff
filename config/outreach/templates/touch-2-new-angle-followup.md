# Touch 2: Touch 2: The New Angle

**Section:** Follow Up Sequence (Touches 2-5)

**When to use it:** Days 7 to 14. Reply on the original thread, keep the subject line.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Re: [Original Subject]

```
Hi [First Name],

Following up with something more useful than a bump.

[New input: a pattern you have seen at a comparable company, a specific observation about their hiring since you last wrote, something that changed in their market.]

Given where [Company Name] is in the post-raise stretch, it seemed worth passing along.

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

The rule for this touch is that it must contain something you did not send in touch one. New information earns a second read. A reminder does not.

## Failure Mode

Restating the value proposition in different words. That is a bump wearing a costume and founders recognise it immediately.
