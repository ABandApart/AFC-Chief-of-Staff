# Template 24: The Direct Executive Email

**Section:** Series B and Beyond

**When to use it:** High-score prospects where you have strong named proof and want to compress the cycle.

## Template Text

**Subject:** [Company Name] and [function]

```
Hi [First Name],

I run [function] on a fractional basis for companies at your stage. Clients include [Client 1], [Client 2] and [Client 3].

Engagements start with a [two week assessment / thirty day diagnostic] and a written recommendation you keep whether or not it goes further than that.

I would like fifteen minutes. Are you the right person for this, or should I be talking to someone else?

[Your Name]

[Title]
```

## Pro Tip

The keep-the-recommendation clause is what makes this convert. It converts the first step from a sales call into a deliverable, which is a much easier internal justification at this size.

## Failure Mode

Sending this without genuinely recognisable client names. Directness without proof reads as presumption at Series B, and the same email that works with three known logos fails without them.
