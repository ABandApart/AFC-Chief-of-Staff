# Template 22: The Readiness Gap

**Section:** Series B and Beyond

**When to use it:** Replaces the compliance and security angle. Use when the company is about to face requirements it is not structured for.

## Template Text

**Subject:** [Enterprise clients / the next round] and what they will ask for

```
Hi [First Name],

Series B usually brings the first wave of [enterprise clients / serious diligence], and with them a set of requirements most growth stage companies are not yet structured to handle: [security questionnaires and SOC 2 / audited financials and revenue recognition / procurement and legal review at a volume nobody staffed for].

I have taken [number] companies through that readiness work as a fractional [function]. [Client] passed [specific milestone] in [timeframe].

Is that on the roadmap for [Company Name] this year?

[Your Name]

[Title]
```

## Pro Tip

Readiness work has a natural end point, which makes it the easiest fractional engagement for a large company to approve. Defined scope, defined finish, no headcount conversation.

## Failure Mode

Claiming readiness experience you supervised rather than ran. This gets tested in the first technical conversation and there is no recovering from it.
