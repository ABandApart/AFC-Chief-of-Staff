# Template 47: The Departure Trigger

**Section:** Re-engagement

**When to use it:** The person who took the seat has left, or a senior leader in your function has departed. The strongest re-engagement available to you.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Saw the change on the [function] side

```
Hi [First Name],

We spoke back in [month] when you were working out [function]. Saw that [role or situation] has changed.

Not going to pretend that is anything other than a hard stretch to be in, particularly with [current context: the board cycle / the number you are carrying this quarter].

If it is useful to have someone hold it while you work out the permanent answer, I am around. Same terms as we discussed, clean exit whenever you have the answer.

If not, genuinely no problem.

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

This is the highest converting message in the entire system and it exists nowhere in vendor outreach material, because vendors do not have a version of it. Set alerts on every company that hired instead of engaging you.

## Failure Mode

Any hint of vindication. You were right and they were wrong is the subtext they will read into this if you give them the smallest opening, and it ends the relationship permanently. Read it back twice.
