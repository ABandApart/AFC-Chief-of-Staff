# Template 04: The Mutual Connection Warm Open

**Section:** Seed Stage

**When to use it:** Shared connection, accelerator cohort, operator community, or prior colleague.

## Template Text

**Subject:** [Mutual Connection] suggested I reach out

```
Hi [First Name],

[Mutual Connection] mentioned [Company Name] raised and is scaling [specific area].

I run [function] fractionally for seed stage companies. Most recently [reference company and one outcome].

At your size the real choice is doing it yourself, hiring a full time leader before you can justify one, or bringing in someone senior on a defined term. I am the third option.

Fifteen minutes this week?

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

Naming all three options is what makes this land. Founders at seed are actively weighing exactly those three and rarely see the third one framed cleanly.

## Failure Mode

Using the connection's name without telling them first. Ask permission, always.
