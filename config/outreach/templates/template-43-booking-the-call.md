# Template 43: Booking the Call

**Section:** Handling the Reply

**When to use it:** Any reply expressing interest or agreeing to talk.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Re: [Original Subject]

```
Hi [First Name],

Good to hear from you.

I have [Day 1] at [Time] or [Day 2] at [Time] open. Either work?

If neither does, send me two that do and I will make one of them work.

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

Offer two specific times rather than a scheduling link. At founder and executive level a link from someone they have not met reads as vendor, and this is the exact moment you cannot afford to slip out of peer positioning. Send the link only after they ask for one or after the first call.

## Failure Mode

Adding material to the email. They already said yes. Anything else you write can only introduce a reason to reconsider.
