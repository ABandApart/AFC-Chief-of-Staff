# Template 16: The Risk Mitigation Angle

**Section:** Series A

**When to use it:** Strong standalone opener, and the best breakup or re-engagement message in the pack.

## Template Text

**Subject:** A common Series A mistake

```
Hi [First Name],

The most common Series A mistake I see is hiring the team before the function has an owner. Ten reps and no VP. Four engineers and nobody setting technical direction. A finance team of one closing books that nobody reviews.

It is not catastrophic. It is just expensive, and it usually takes two quarters to unwind.

I run [function] fractionally, and most of my engagements exist either to prevent that gap or to repair it.

Have you worked out who owns [function] once the [role] hires land?

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

This one works at day sixty as the re-engagement touch. By then the hires have landed, the gap is visible, and the question at the end stops being hypothetical.

## Failure Mode

Delivering the mistake as a lecture. Name it, size it, then hand them a question. Three sentences, not a paragraph of diagnosis.
