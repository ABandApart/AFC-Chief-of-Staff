# Template 11: The Board Pressure Angle

**Section:** Series A

**When to use it:** Weeks four through ten after the announcement, ahead of the first post-raise board meeting.

## Template Text

**Subject:** First board meeting after the A

```
Hi [First Name],

The first post Series A board meeting tends to surface the same short list of questions: [pipeline coverage and forecast confidence / gross margin by segment / unit economics that survive at 3x].

Having answers is one thing. Having someone who owns those numbers between meetings is a different thing, and it is usually the gap.

I run [function] fractionally for Series A companies and a large part of the work is exactly this. Build the reporting the board actually wants, then run the function that produces the numbers underneath it.

[Client] walked into their second board meeting with [specific: a forecast they could defend / a model that held up in diligence] after [timeframe].

Is that on your radar yet?

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

This is the highest converting angle for fractional CFOs and close behind for CROs. The board meeting is a fixed date on their calendar, which gives your email a deadline you did not have to invent.

## Failure Mode

Implying the board is unhappy. You have no idea whether they are. You are naming a predictable agenda, not a problem.
