# Template 02: The Investor Alignment Angle

**Section:** Seed Stage

**When to use it:** When you have genuine working history inside the lead investor's portfolio.

## Template Text

**Subject:** [Investor Name] portfolio and fractional [function]

```
Hi [First Name],

Saw [Investor Name] led your round. I work with [number] companies across their portfolio as fractional [function], including [Portfolio Company].

At [Portfolio Company] I [specific outcome: built the revenue function from first hire to [X] ARR / closed the books and had them audit ready ahead of their Series A].

Same stage, same shape of problem. Worth a short call?

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

This is the highest converting angle available to a fractional and the one most students cannot use yet. If you are not already on a fund's bench, the correct first move is the platform or talent partner at the fund, not the founder.

## Failure Mode

Manufacturing the connection. Founders talk to their investors weekly. This gets checked.
