# Template 23: Efficiency at Scale

**Section:** Series B and Beyond

**When to use it:** Strong fractional COO and fractional CFO angle. Works anytime after the headcount from the raise has landed.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Doing more with the same team

```
Hi [First Name],

The pattern I see most often at Series B is that the team doubles and output does not. Coordination cost climbs, processes that were informal start failing quietly, and the leadership team spends more time managing than deciding.

I run [function] fractionally and a good share of that work is exactly this. [One sentence on the proprietary method you apply.]

[Client] went from [X] to [Y] people while holding [specific efficiency metric].

Is operational leverage on your leadership agenda right now?

[Your Name]

[Title]
```

## Pro Tip

This is the best template in the pack for a re-engagement touch, because the symptom it names only becomes visible after the post-raise hiring has actually landed.

## Failure Mode

Describing the symptom for a full paragraph before saying anything about yourself. Three sentences of diagnosis, then move.
