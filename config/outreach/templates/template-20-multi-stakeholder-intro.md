# Template 20: The Multi Stakeholder Introduction

**Section:** Series B and Beyond

**When to use it:** When you cannot identify the decision owner, or when you know several people will be involved.

## Template Text

**Subject:** For [First Name] and the [Department] team

```
Hi [First Name],

At [Company Name]'s stage I assume a fractional [function] engagement touches you, the board, and whoever owns vendor contracts. So rather than pitch, here is the short version and you can decide whether it is worth circulating.

I run [function] on defined terms for companies between Series B and C. Most recently [Client 1] and [Client 2], where I [specific outcome].

I can provide references, an MSA and insurance documentation upfront so nothing stalls in review.

If someone else is better placed for this conversation, I am happy to go to them directly.

[Your Name]

[Title]
```

## Pro Tip

The paperwork line is not filler. At this size the most common reason a fractional engagement dies is that it stalls in legal for six weeks and the urgency evaporates. Removing that objection before it is raised is worth more than another proof point.

## Failure Mode

Skipping the final line. Offering to be redirected is what makes this template work, and it is the sentence students cut because it feels like giving up the contact.
