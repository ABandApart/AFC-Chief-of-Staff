# Template 21: The Expansion Timing Play

**Section:** Series B and Beyond

**When to use it:** Days seven to fourteen, when the announcement names an expansion the capital is funding.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Before [Company Name] expands into [market or region]

```
Hi [First Name],

Read that the Series B goes toward [expansion initiative].

The pattern I have seen repeatedly is that expansion gets funded and staffed before anyone senior owns it, and the first two quarters go to relearning things the company already knows somewhere else.

I ran [function] through [comparable expansion] at [Client]. [Specific pitfall avoided, or outcome with a number.]

Would a conversation about sequencing be useful before you ramp?

[Your Name]

[Title]
```

## Pro Tip

Sequencing is the right word and worth keeping. It offers judgment about order of operations rather than a resource, which is what senior buyers are actually short of.

## Failure Mode

Waiting until the expansion is underway. This template has a genuine expiry date. Once the country lead is hired the opening is gone.
