# Template 48: The Trigger Event Re-engagement

**Section:** Re-engagement

**When to use it:** A product launch, market expansion, partnership, second raise, or a senior hire in an adjacent function.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Saw [Company Name]'s news

```
Hi [First Name],

We talked briefly back in [month]. Saw that [Company Name] recently [trigger event].

That usually pulls [function] into a different shape, because [specific implication you have watched play out].

Curious whether it is on your radar now. No agenda beyond that.

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

A second funding round is the most reliable trigger in this template and the one students forget to watch for. It resets the entire arc, and you are re-entering as a known name rather than a cold one.

## Failure Mode

Sending this on a trigger that has nothing to do with your function. If you cannot finish the because clause honestly, the trigger is not yours to use.
