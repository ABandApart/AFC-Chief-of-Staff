# Template 06: The Case Study Lead

**Section:** Seed Stage

**When to use it:** Touch three, days fourteen to thirty, when you have same-stage proof.

## Template Text

**Subject:** How [Similar Company] handled [function] after their raise

```
Hi [First Name],

[Similar Company] was roughly where [Company Name] is now, about [timeframe] ago. Freshly raised, trying to [goal], nobody owning [function].

I came in fractionally and [specific outcome with a number] within [timeframe].

I wrote up how it worked. Want me to send it over?

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

Narrow proof beats broad credibility here. Same function, same stage, a number that resembles the number they are chasing. A general capabilities deck underperforms badly at this touch.

## Failure Mode

Attaching the case study to the first email. Offering it and getting a yes is the engagement. Sending it unrequested is a brochure.
