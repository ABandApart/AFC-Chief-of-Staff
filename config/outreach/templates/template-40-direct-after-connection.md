# Template 40: The Direct After Connection

**Section:** LinkedIn

**When to use it:** High-score prospects where a specific public signal exists and you want to compress the cycle.

## Template Text

**Subject:** LinkedIn message, no subject line

```
Appreciate the connection, [First Name].

Being direct rather than working around to it: I run [function] fractionally, and from the outside it looks like [specific observation: you are building the team ahead of the leader / the [Role Title] seat has been open a while].

That is usually the point where this kind of engagement makes sense, either alongside the search or ahead of it.

Open to ten minutes? If the timing is off, no pressure at all.

Also worth saying plainly: I am not a recruiter and I am not pitching you a candidate.
```

## Pro Tip

The last line looks unnecessary until you have watched a founder file three of these messages a week. Keep it. It costs one sentence and it prevents the single most common misread.

## Failure Mode

Sending this as a first message to someone who has never heard of you. It needs the connection, and it works far better after the engagement sequence below.
