# Template 46: The Budget Objection

**Section:** Handling the Reply

**When to use it:** "We cannot afford it right now," or "we have no budget for this."

## Template Text

**Subject:** Re: [Original Subject]

```
Hi [First Name],

Fair. Two numbers worth putting side by side.

A full time [Role Title] at your stage runs [range] fully loaded, plus [search fee], plus ramp before they are productive. A fractional engagement is [structure: days per month on a defined term] and it stops when you say it stops.

The other number is what the function not being owned costs per month, which at your size is usually the larger of the two.

Happy to size either one honestly, including telling you if the answer is wait a quarter.

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

The last clause is the entire template. Offering to talk them out of it is the most credible thing a fractional can say, and it converts better than any argument about value.

## Failure Mode

Discounting. The first engagement you discount sets the rate for every conversation that follows, including the ones with people they refer you to.
