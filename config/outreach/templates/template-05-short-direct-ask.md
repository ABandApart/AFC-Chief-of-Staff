# Template 05: The Short and Direct Ask

**Section:** Seed Stage

**When to use it:** Strong fit, high score, and you want to move fast.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Quick question, [First Name]

```
Hi [First Name],

Congrats on the raise.

Curious how you are handling [function] right now. At seed the answer is usually you, and usually at the wrong hours.

I run [function] fractionally for [number] companies at your stage. Happy to share what is working for them either way.

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

The question does the work. It is genuinely open, it costs nothing to answer, and the answer tells you whether there is an engagement here.

## Failure Mode

Answering your own question inside the email. Ask it and stop.
