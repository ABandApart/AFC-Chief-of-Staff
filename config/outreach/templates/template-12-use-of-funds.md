# Template 12: The Use of Funds Alignment

**Section:** Series A

**When to use it:** Days seven to fourteen, once the announcement has named where the money is going.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Your [announced priority] plans

```
Hi [First Name],

Read that the Series A goes toward [announced use: doubling the go to market team / expanding into [market] / launching the enterprise tier].

Every one of those carries a leadership requirement that almost never gets funded on the same timeline as the plan itself. You can hire ten reps, but the number does not move until somebody owns the motion they are selling into.

I have run [function] through that exact expansion at [Similar Company]. [Result with a number.]

Happy to walk you through how they sequenced it, whether or not anything comes of it with me.

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

The last line is the whole email. Offering the sequencing unconditionally is what separates this from a pitch, and at Series A it converts better than a meeting request.

## Failure Mode

Guessing at the use of funds. If the announcement did not state it, this is the wrong template.
