# Template 01: The Congrats Plus Relevance Opener

**Section:** Seed Stage

**When to use it:** Within seven days of the announcement. This is touch one.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Congrats on the round, [First Name]

```
Hi [First Name],

Saw [Company Name] closed your seed. Congrats.

Noticed you are [specific observation: hiring your first two AEs / building out the data team / moving into [market]].

I have run [function] at seed stage [industry] companies through exactly that stretch. The thing that usually breaks first is [specific operating problem], and it tends to break about a quarter after the raise rather than right away.

Not pitching anything. If it is ever useful to compare notes, I am around.

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

Pull the observation from your five point research. Job postings, the announcement itself, and the founder's own LinkedIn post are the best sources.

## Failure Mode

Adding a calendar link. There is no ask in this message. If you put one in, it stops being touch one and becomes a cold pitch with a congratulations attached.
