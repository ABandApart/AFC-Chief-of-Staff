# Template 41: The InMail With Proof

**Section:** LinkedIn

**When to use it:** When you cannot get a connection accepted and the prospect scores high enough to spend an InMail credit.

## Template Text

**Subject:** [Mutual contact or peer company name]

```
Hi [First Name],

I run [function] fractionally for [number] recently funded companies, including [Named Company in their space].

Given [Company Name]'s round, it seemed worth a short conversation.

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

InMail displays a subject line, so the subject carries most of the open decision. A recognisable company or contact name in the subject outperforms any descriptive phrase.

## Failure Mode

Spending InMail credits on prospects who did not score high. InMail is the most expensive and lowest converting channel here and should be the last resort, not the volume play.
