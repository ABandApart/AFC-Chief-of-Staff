# Template 09: The Growth Mandate Angle

**Section:** Series A

**When to use it:** General opener when the function is under obvious strain but no req is posted yet.

## Template Text

**Subject:** Scaling [function] after the Series A

```
Hi [First Name],

Series A is usually where the [function] decision gets forced, because the pressure to scale [revenue / delivery / margin] stops being something anyone can absorb personally.

I run [function] fractionally for Series A companies, most recently [Client 1] and [Client 2]. The pattern is almost always the same. The function was held together by [the founder / a capable generalist / an agency] and it held right up until volume tripled.

At [Client 1] I [specific outcome with a number] inside [timeframe].

Is [function] something [Company Name] is investing in this quarter, or is it still sitting behind the next round of hires?

[Your Name]
```

## Pro Tip

The closing double question is the mechanic. It gives them a true and easy answer either way, and both answers tell you where they are in the arc.

## Failure Mode

Naming the current arrangement in a way that identifies a person. Say a capable generalist, not their head of ops by name.
