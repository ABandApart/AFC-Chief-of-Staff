# Template 17: The Scale Transition

**Section:** Series B and Beyond

**When to use it:** General opener when the company has clearly outgrown the way the function was run at Series A.

## Template Text

**Subject:** [Function] at [Company Name]'s scale

```
Hi [First Name],

Series B is usually where the operating approach that got a company here starts to strain. Not because anything was done wrong. [Function] at eighty people and [function] at three hundred are different jobs carrying the same title.

I have run [function] through that specific transition at [Client 1] and [Client 2], both at your stage or above. At [Client 1], [specific outcome with a number] over [timeframe].

Is your team working through that shift now, or is it already handled?

[Your Name]

[Title]
```

## Pro Tip

The second sentence is doing the diplomatic work. It moves the problem from a person to a stage, which matters enormously when the function already has a leader in it.

## Failure Mode

Any phrasing that implies the current leader has been outgrown. You do not know who reads this email or who forwards it, and at this size the incumbent almost certainly outranks your sender.
